Lithium batteries with Bluetooth

Free. No monitor needed: the battery’s own BMS gives its charge, the current in or out, power, every cell’s voltage, temperatures and alarms. Read-only: Voltsure never changes the battery’s settings or switches it on or off. The BMS keeps no history, so readings are saved while it’s connected.

The encryption key from VictronConnect is optional. With it, Voltsure also reads the device’s Instant Readout broadcasts, so it keeps updating while another phone is connected. See Support for where to find it.

Your iPhone, iPad and Mac

  • iPhone or iPad with iOS 18.5 or later, and Bluetooth.
  • Mac with Apple silicon.
  • CarPlay, wired or wireless, with Voltsure Pro.

Not yet

Voltsure doesn’t yet read lithium batteries with other BMS boards (JK, Daly, LiTime and others), Renogy DC-DC chargers, solar controllers and shunts (a charger’s BT-2 shows as BT-TH-… too; Voltsure says it isn’t a battery), temperature sensors, gas level sensors, heaters or fridges yet. If there’s a device you’d like to see, email us its make and model.
