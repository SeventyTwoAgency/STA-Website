Every monitor, every battery

Mix your monitors. Add as many as you like.

Voltsure reads BM2, BM6 and BM7 Bluetooth battery monitors, including the BM200 and BM300 Pro, side by side in one app. Most monitor apps work with only their own brand. Support for the BM7 / BM300 Pro and the intAct Battery Guard is experimental: built from their documented Bluetooth protocol, and not yet tested on a real monitor.

Put one on the leisure battery and another on the starter, then add a second van, a caravan or a boat. There’s no limit on how many batteries you add, in Free or Pro. Each one keeps its own name, battery type and alert level, and every connected monitor reads at the same time.

  • BM2Battery Monitor and Li Battery Monitor: voltage and history.
  • BM6Also sold as the BM200. Adds temperature and the monitor’s own status.
  • BM7Also sold as the BM300 Pro. Temperature and status, like the BM6. Experimental: not yet tested on a real monitor.
  • RebadgesintAct Battery Guard (experimental), and monitors sold under other brands and names.
  • No limitAs many monitors as you like, in any mix.

Features

Everything your batteries are telling you

See every battery at a glance, catch problems before they leave you flat, and look back at how your setup is really performing.

Every battery at a glance

Live voltage, an estimate of the charge left, and whether each battery is charging, rising, stable, falling or low.

Alerts before it goes flat

A notification when a battery stays below its alert level for five minutes, even through Focus. Sensible defaults for lead-acid, AGM and lithium (LiFePO4). Alerts work while your iPhone is in Bluetooth range; if a falling battery goes out of range, Voltsure tells you alerts have stopped.

History that catches up

Your monitor logs a reading every two minutes. Voltsure downloads it when it reconnects, so charts fill in after time away.

CarPlay PRO

Check your batteries on the dashboard before you set off, without reaching for your phone.

Widgets PRO

Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets put your battery levels one glance away.

Vehicles & export PRO

Group batteries by vehicle, with number plates, and export your readings as CSV whenever you like.

Your batteries, your solar, your whole setup

Voltsure 3.8 adds the rest of your power system, with Victron Smart devices read live, and puts it all on your dashboard.

Your whole power system PRO

Add solar chargers, shunts, DC-DC and mains chargers beside your batteries, and see how they connect: as a list, an energy-flow map, or a map of your vehicle from above.

Victron Smart devices PRO

Victron Smart solar chargers, shunts, Orion DC-DC and mains chargers show their live readings, with no extra hardware: Voltsure reads what they already broadcast, using each device’s key from VictronConnect.

Every device in one place PRO

A Devices tab with every battery, charger and shunt, grouped by vehicle: van, car, caravan, motorhome or boat.

Solar in, power used PRO

History → Energy shows solar in, energy used and the balance for every day of the week, from your solar charger and shunt.

More on the dashboard PRO

CarPlay lists your Victron devices under their vehicle, after its batteries, with their live readings.

Alerts you can rely on

Low-voltage alerts are Time Sensitive, so they come through Focus, and Voltsure tells you if a falling battery goes out of range.

Honest about the numbers

Clear status, without pretending

These monitors measure voltage, not current. Voltsure is upfront about that: charge is shown as an estimate, labelled “under load” or “settling” when the voltage reads low or high, and the status tells you what the voltage is actually doing.

Open any battery to see its last 24 hours, 7 days or 30 days, with charging periods shaded and your low-voltage alert marked.

  • ChargingA charger, alternator or solar is supplying power.
  • RisingVoltage has risen over the last two minutes.
  • StableVoltage is holding steady.
  • FallingSomething is likely drawing power.
  • LowBelow the battery’s low-voltage alert level.
