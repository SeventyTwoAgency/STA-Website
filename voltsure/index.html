Every monitor, every battery

Mix your monitors. Add as many as you like.

Voltsure reads BM2, BM6 and BM7 Bluetooth battery monitors, including the BM200 and BM300 Pro, side by side in one app. Most monitor apps work with only their own brand. Support for the BM7 and rebadged monitors is experimental: built from published notes and not yet tested on every model.

Put one on the leisure battery and another on the starter, then add a second van, a caravan or a boat. There’s no limit on how many batteries you add, in Free or Pro. Each one keeps its own name, battery type and alert level, and every connected monitor reads at the same time.

  • BM2Battery Monitor and Li Battery Monitor: voltage and history.
  • BM6Also sold as the BM200. Adds temperature and the monitor’s own status.
  • BM7Also sold as the BM300 Pro. Temperature and status, like the BM6. Experimental.
  • RebadgesintAct Battery Guard and other rebranded BM2, BM6 and BM7 monitors. Experimental.
  • No limitAs many monitors as you like, in any mix.

Features

Everything your batteries are telling you

See every battery at a glance, catch problems before they leave you flat, and look back at how your setup is really performing.

Every battery at a glance

Live voltage, an estimate of the charge left, and whether each battery is charging, rising, stable, falling or low.

Alerts before it goes flat

A notification when a battery stays below its alert level for five minutes. Sensible defaults for lead-acid, AGM and lithium (LiFePO4).

History that catches up

Your monitor logs a reading every two minutes. Voltsure downloads it when it reconnects, so charts fill in after time away.

CarPlay PRO

Check your batteries on the dashboard before you set off, without reaching for your phone.

Widgets PRO

Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets put your battery levels one glance away.

Vehicles & export PRO

Group batteries by vehicle, with number plates, and export your readings as CSV whenever you like.

Honest about the numbers

Clear status, without pretending

These monitors measure voltage, not current. Voltsure is upfront about that: charge is shown as an estimate, labelled “under load” or “settling” when the voltage reads low or high, and the status tells you what the voltage is actually doing.

Open any battery to see its last 24 hours, 7 days or 30 days, with charging periods shaded and your low-voltage alert marked.

  • ChargingA charger, alternator or solar is supplying power.
  • RisingVoltage has risen over the last two minutes.
  • StableVoltage is holding steady.
  • FallingSomething is likely drawing power.
  • LowBelow the battery’s low-voltage alert level.
