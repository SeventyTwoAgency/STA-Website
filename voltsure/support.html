Common questions

Which monitors work with Voltsure?

BM2, BM6 and BM200 Bluetooth battery monitors, tested with Voltsure.

The BM7 (sold as the BM300 Pro), the intAct Battery Guard and other rebadged BM2, BM6 and BM7 monitors are experimental: support is built from other people’s notes on how they work, and hasn’t been tested on one yet. Voltsure marks them “Experimental” when you add them. If yours doesn’t work as it should, please tell us which monitor it is and what you see.

Monitor under a name Voltsure doesn’t recognise? In Add Battery, look under “Other Bluetooth devices”: Voltsure works out which kind it is once connected.

You can add as many as you like and mix models freely, in Free and Pro. Every connected monitor reads at the same time.

The app can’t find my monitor
  • Check Bluetooth is on.
  • Make sure the monitor is fitted to the battery.
  • Stay within about 10 m of it.
  • Close the monitor maker’s app, or disconnect the monitor there.
  • A monitor connects to only one phone or iPad at a time. If another device nearby is connected to it, close the app there or turn off its Bluetooth.
Why is the charge an estimate?

These monitors measure voltage, not current. Charge is worked out from the voltage and is most accurate after the battery has rested with nothing running for an hour. Under load the true charge is at least the figure shown; just after charging it is at most the figure shown.

What does the status mean?

The status is estimated from battery voltage. Charging and discharging can’t be confirmed without measuring current.

  • Charging At the battery type’s charging voltage, so a charger, alternator or solar is supplying power. It shows for 5 minutes after the last burst, as smart alternators charge in bursts.
  • Rising The voltage has risen over the last two minutes. Charging is likely, though a load switching off does the same.
  • Stable The voltage is holding steady. A charger and a load may both be running.
  • Falling The voltage has fallen over the last two minutes, so something is likely drawing power.
  • Low Below the battery’s low-voltage alert level.
  • Unknown Not enough readings yet, as with a battery just added. The status needs ten minutes of stored readings, or two minutes of live ones.

Lithium (LiFePO4) voltage barely moves while charging or discharging, so a lithium battery often shows Stable.

I didn’t get a low-voltage alert

Alerts need notifications allowed for Voltsure, and the app must not be force-quit: iOS only reconnects to monitors in the background while the app is still running.

There are gaps in my history

Monitors keep the last few days of readings. The app downloads them when it reconnects, so gaps fill in once you’re back in range, as long as you return within a few days.

How do I restore Voltsure Pro?

Voltsure Pro is a one-time purchase tied to your Apple ID and shared with your family through Family Sharing. On a new device, open the Voltsure Pro screen in the app and tap Restore Purchase.

Does Voltsure collect my data?

No. No account, no servers, no location, no analytics. It works fully offline. See the privacy policy.
